   (`live_streaming.rs:365`), `summarize_log` (`virtualization.rs:197`), and
   `causality_status_for_engine` (`live_streaming.rs:181`) are implemented and tested (22 + 25 tests),

   "open the log per session_id" and "derive EventSummary from the engine" is an architecture decision
   under ADR-0029 §3.2/§3.4. `live_streaming` (723 L) and `virtualization` are orphan leaves.
