# Verification Lab Relation to Roadmap

This proposal is intentionally subordinate to the existing reconstruction roadmap.

It introduces auxiliary milestone identifiers (`SANDBOX-S0`, `DOG-*`, `PORTABLE-P*`) so sandbox work can be tracked without renumbering official reconstruction milestones.

Rules:

- REC-C0 closure criteria remain unchanged.
- REC-C1 may consume DOG-001/DOG-002.
- REC-C2 may consume DOG-003.
- M4 may consume KERNEL/DOG-004.
- PORTABLE-P* remains future work until its adoption gates are met.
